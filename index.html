Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

This Privacy Policy applies to the Meta / Instagram application TOPWINNER operated in connection with the “大赢家” content publishing tools (“we”, “us”).

1. What we access

When you authorize TOPWINNER, we may access Instagram account identifiers and permissions you grant (for example content publishing related scopes) solely to upload and publish Reels/posts that you choose to publish through our tools.

2. How we use data

We use authorization tokens and media you provide only to publish content to your Instagram professional account on your instruction. We do not sell personal data. We do not use Instagram data for unrelated advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens may be stored locally on the operator’s device or secure configuration files used by the publishing scripts. Tokens are not shared with third parties except Meta/Instagram as required to complete API calls.

4. Third parties

Publishing uses Meta Instagram Graph APIs. Meta’s own privacy policy also applies to data processed by Meta.

5. Your choices

You may revoke TOPWINNER’s access at any time in Instagram / Facebook settings → Apps and Websites (or Business integrations).

6. Contact

For privacy questions related to TOPWINNER, contact the app administrator via the Meta Developer dashboard for this app.
