Privacy Policy (TikTok)

Last updated: 2026-07-25

This Privacy Policy applies to the TikTok application TOPWINNER used with the “大赢家” desktop publishing tools (“we”, “us”).

1. What we access

When you authorize TOPWINNER, we may access TikTok account identifiers and permissions you grant (for example Login Kit and Content Posting scopes) solely to publish videos you choose to publish through our tools.

2. How we use data

We use authorization tokens and media you provide only to publish content to your TikTok account on your instruction. We do not sell personal data. We do not use TikTok data for unrelated advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens and refresh tokens may be stored locally on the operator’s device or in secure configuration files used by the publishing scripts. Tokens are not shared with third parties except TikTok as required to complete API calls.

4. Third parties

Publishing uses TikTok Open APIs. TikTok’s own privacy policy also applies to data processed by TikTok.

5. Your choices

You may revoke TOPWINNER’s access at any time in TikTok settings for connected apps, or by deleting local token files and re-authorizing only if needed.

6. Contact

For privacy questions related to TOPWINNER on TikTok, contact the app administrator via the TikTok for Developers dashboard for this app.
